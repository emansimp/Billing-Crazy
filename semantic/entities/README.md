# entities
Business objects and how the source systems join.

- `customer_crosswalk.csv` - one row per customer (30). Generated by `scripts/export_crosswalk.py` from `stg.v_customer_crosswalk`.
  Salesforce `AccountId` is the customer key. Joins: Stripe `customer_id` = `StripeCustomerId__c`; NetSuite `Customer Internal ID` =
  `NetSuiteCustomerInternalId__c`; contracts via `contract_register.salesforce_account_id`; product accounts via `product_account_mapping`
  (verified mappings only, `|`-separated when a customer has more than one).
- Flag columns: `missing_*` (identifier absent), `*_not_in_*` (identifier present but never appears in the other system),
  `any_identifier_missing`. Current gap: **Willow Passage has no verified product account** - its usage is unknown, not zero.
- `unassigned_workspaces.csv` - product accounts with no verified owner. They are never assigned to a customer by elimination.
